Basic Asset Usage Rules

These are a basic list of rules if you want to use/post any of my stuff publicly, these rules do not substitute for an actual license.

  • Publicly listed resources and projects and the like can be used for personal use without permission from me.
  • The user cannot monetize directly (e.g. paywalls) from publicly free resources. Indirect means of monetization (e.g. Advertising) are excluded.
  • If you paid for access for any of my publicly available resources, you demand a refund.
    • This does not include commissions that I do. The commissioner still has to pay for that.
  • All resources must be used in “good faith”, meaning the user must not use said resources to polarize, defame, or cause unneeded discourse or drama.
  • Any publicly listed project that uses my resources (e.g. characters) for anything deemed Not Safe For Work (NSFW) cannot be shared publicly without permission.
  • The user must not redistribute unmodified projects (aka “reposting” or “stealing”) without permission.
  • Any modified version of a resource must have proper attribution, permission from me is optional, but preferred.
  • I reserve the right to send a take down notice on other projects with my resources at any time, including unofficial redistributions.
  • Do not claim any of my works as made by anybody but me (don’t falsely credit things PLEASE, other people need to be credited too!).
  • These guidelines are just simple usage guidelines, not a replacement for an actual license (IF YOU DIDN'T READ THE TOP.).
